❖ Biography ❖


Beginning

In today’s fast changing world, information technology and artificial intelligence are raising numerous new challenges for science and society, and innovative interdisciplinary research is needed to find answers to these challenges. As a bioinformatics researcher and practitioner of biomedical informatics, I have always equipped myself with the necessary interdisciplinary backgrounds to help me bridge the gaps between biology and information technology. Besides my formal education, I have also taken up a large amount of self-learning to equip myself with the right technological knowledge that could support real world applications.

College

Graduated from National Chung Hsing University as a Bachelor’s degree holder, I started my bioinformatics research projects which are mainly related to the clustering of human microbiome data using machine learning techniques. I have published my research works in various bioinformatics-related publications and conferences such as the 18th IEEE International Conference on Bioinformatics and Bioengineering (BIBE) and the International Journal of Uncertainty and Innovation.

Graduated School

I pursued my graduate studies in the field of biomedical informatics and artificial intelligence at the National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (formerly National Yang Ming University) where I learned more about the Python programming language, deep learning, biostatistics and translational biomedical informatics. I also took part in a summer student program at the National Institutes of Health (NIH) where I learned and applied the use of computing techniques in solving real-world problems. 

My master’s thesis work is on the development of an automated pipeline for data cleansing, data validation and feature extraction to prepare clinical health record data for machine learning modeling. Although most of the work was focused in the early stages of data cleansing and preprocessing automation, I was able to gain a lot of hands-on experience on software development, data engineering, and analytical workflow development.

Work & Research

After graduation, I set my foot on the data science and artificial intelligence field for healthcare applications and joined Linkou Chang Gung Memorial Hospital as a research assistant. By building upon what I have learned from my master’s thesis work, I developed more advanced automated pipelines for data processing of the ICU monitoring data, AI model training and clinical risk prediction. One of the practical projects is the early warning prediction system for critical inpatient events by leveraging automated data preprocessing and machine learning model training.

Since healthcare and clinical data are very sensitive and require strict privacy protection as well as involve multiple stakeholders from different departments, I am able to gain valuable experiences by liaising with other departments, physicians and hospital staff to ensure smooth data coordination, HIS data access and system integration.

Future

With the increased capabilities of the new generations of generative AI, it is now possible to rethink and redesign the way we worked and boost new innovations for the future. Currently, I am actively exploring the possibilities of using the modern AI tools to enhance and re-engineer my past works. I hope to contribute my experiences in biomedical informatics, machine learning and the practical AI implementations to the next AI or data science roles.

起點

在近代社會發展中，資訊科技乃至 AI 技術與不同領域知識的結合應用，已逐漸成為推動革新的核心力量。雖然我過去所鑽研的專業，是生物相關的科系，但我始終重視著跨領域能力的培養，因此除了本科課程外，還會利用額外時間選修程式語言、資訊安全的課程，並參與資訊技術相關的工作坊，讓自己具備能於現實應用的專業技能。

大學的基礎

我大學時期就讀國立中興大學，而第一份與生物資訊相關的研究，也是在那時展開，主要是嘗試利用機器學習方法，對人體微菌叢資料（human microbiome data）作分群與分析。而該研究結果已發表在 18th IEEE International Conference on Bioinformatics and Bioengineering 中。後續另一份相似研究，則更進一步著重於腸道菌相種類與 BMI 預測的關係，結果也有提交於 International Journal of Uncertainty and Innovation 進行發表。

研究所精進

畢業後，為了近一步深耕跨生物資訊領域的技術，我選擇進入陽明大學（現陽明交通大學）的生物醫學資訊研究所，持續精進資料分析與人工智慧相關技能，像是 Python 程式設計、深度學習、生物統計、轉譯醫學資訊等，並參與了暑期至美國國家衛生研究院（NIH）的實習計畫，進一步加強自己將資訊工具應用於問題解決方案的技巧，同時為自己針對臨床醫療紀錄進行自動化清理、分析工具開發為主題的論文研究奠定了基礎。

儘管當時的開發成果，仍只能達成初步的資料品質改善，並將臨床紀錄轉換成接近適用於機器學習訓練材料，但我依然在過程學獲得不少自動化程式開發的經驗，以及資料分析概念，同時熟悉多種分析相關套件的應用。

職涯與研究

在我的第一份工作，於林口長庚醫院擔任醫師研究助理的期間，因為研究需求，我更進一步這些開發經歷進行發揮，以原先資料處理流的概念做延伸，開發出一套更完善的自動化 ICU 監測資料清理，與 AI 模型訓練的流程工具，並將產出的訓練結果串接至臨床，建立針對住院患者，臨床重大突發事件的預警系統。

另外在工作其間，鑑於可用於研究的醫療紀錄具高度隱私性，院內各部門對資料管理的分工結構複雜，我除了進行臨床資料研究外，也還要隨醫師與工作夥伴們，參與並了解與各部門間的協調與串接任務，包括臨床 HIS 系統的資料取得，以及相關院內系統的結構架接等，進一步累積了實務上的醫療資訊系統整合經驗。

未來展望

隨著近年生成式 AI 工具的快速崛起，帶動了各領域在資料應用與系統設計上的思維革新，雖然淡化了程式開發的門檻界線，卻也同時創造了更多跨領域合作的新契機。我自己也積極嘗試透過 AI 輔助，重構過去的開發成果，藉以將新技術融入工作流程，希望未來能以既有的生醫資訊，與人工智慧經驗為基石，持續挑戰更多不同的可能性。
